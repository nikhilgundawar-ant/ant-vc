ant.

Set up ant. for VCs in about ten minutes

ant. is a chief of staff for your fund that lives in the Claude app. It reads your email, calendar, notes and CRM, and gives you one private page each morning: what to decide, what to send, who owes you what, and prep before every meeting.

10 min to the first brief 2 tools required 3 questions 0 emails sent without your tap
Before you start

What you need

Required

The Claude app

  • Signed in, with plugins and scheduled tasks available on your plan
  • The desktop app is best. Research on the people you meet runs only there.
Required

Email and calendar

  • Gmail and Google Calendar
  • Each asks you to sign in once
Recommended

Notes, CRM, Drive

  • Meeting notes: Granola, Zoom, Fathom, Fireflies or Otter
  • CRM: Affinity, Attio, HubSpot, Salesforce, Airtable, Notion, or a Google Sheet
  • Google Drive, for empathy maps
Install

Six steps, in order

  1. Download the plugin 1 min

    Open the latest release on GitHub and download ant-vc.plugin.

    Latest release on GitHub

  2. Accept it in Claude 1 min

    Open the file in the Claude app, or drop it into a chat. A plugin card appears. Tap Accept.

  3. Start setup 10 sec

    In a new chat, type:

    set up ant.

  4. Connect your tools 3 min

    ant. shows a Connect card for each tool it can use. Tap Connect on the ones you use and skip the rest. Then tap Done.

    GmailGoogle CalendarGranolaCRMGoogle DriveSlack (read only)

    Already connected but switched off in this chat? Turn it on from the chat's connector menu.

  5. Answer three questions 1 min

    Your role (partner, principal, associate, analyst, platform, finance and IR, or solo GP), your thesis in two lines (ant. drafts it from your email), and when you want your brief (07:00 is the default).

  6. Approve the two scheduled checks 1 min

    ant. creates two scheduled tasks: ant. runs every hour on weekdays, and ant. weekend runs three times a day on weekends. Your first brief and your ant. Home page arrive about ten minutes after you start.

    Do this or the checks will wait for you: open Scheduled tasks, pick "ant." and "ant. weekend", and switch on Automatically approve. A guard still blocks anything except reading your tools, updating Home, CRM changes you approved, holds on your own calendar and saving drafts.

How it works

One loop, every hour

Nothing runs on an Anthill server. Each check happens inside your own Claude account, and everything it builds lands on one private page only you can open.

Your tools

Email, calendar, meeting notes, CRM. Read through connectors you approved.

Hourly check

Finds promises, stale deals, pitches, founder updates and LP calls. A guard blocks sending and anything not on its list.

ant. Home

Your private page: Today, Pipeline, People, Portfolio, LPs, Reminders. Every line links to its source.

You tap

Done, Approve, Skip, Send, Remind me. Every tap has Undo. Emails go only after Send, then Send now.

the next check applies what you approved, then reads again

Your data

What ant. can and cannot do

Reads

  • Email, calendar and meeting notes
  • Your CRM
  • Public LinkedIn profiles of people you meet, in the desktop app

Writes, with Undo

  • Email drafts, never sent for you
  • CRM fields and notes you approved
  • Holds on your own calendar, nobody invited

Never

  • Sends an email without your two taps
  • Deletes anything
  • Sends your data to Anthill or anyone else

To stop everything, say "turn off ant.". It stops the checks and wipes its data.

If something is off

Quick fixes

The checks keep asking me to approve

Open Scheduled tasks, pick "ant." and "ant. weekend", and switch on Automatically approve. If your organization does not allow it, the checks still run when you approve each one.

My drafts are not in Gmail

Some Gmail connectors cannot save drafts. Then drafts wait in ant. Home, one tap from a filled-in Gmail message. Connect a Gmail tool that saves drafts later and it just works.

My CRM is a Google Sheet

ant. reads the sheet and suggests changes. It cannot write to a sheet, so tap Copy on a change and paste it in.

No empathy maps on my prep cards

Research runs only when you open Home in the Claude desktop app, because it uses the app's browser to read public LinkedIn profiles.

Setup stopped halfway

Type "continue ant. setup". It finds what is already done and picks up from there. It never makes a second Home.

I use Claude Code instead

Run /plugin marketplace add nikhilgundawar-ant/ant-vc then /plugin install ant-vc@anthill, then say "set up ant.".
