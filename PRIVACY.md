# ant. for VCs: privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026. This policy covers the ant-vc plugin for Claude, made by Anthill AI.

## In short

- ant. runs inside your own Claude account. Anthill AI runs no server for it and receives none of your data.
- Nothing is sent, shared or deleted without your tap.
- You can stop ant. and wipe its data at any time.

## What ant. reads

- Your email, calendar, meeting notes and CRM, through the Claude connectors you choose to approve.
- Google Drive and Slack only if you connect them. Slack is read only.
- Public LinkedIn profiles of the people in your external meetings, when ant. Home is open in the Claude desktop app (or when you ask in chat). ant. reads them in the Claude app's browser, where you are signed in. It never sends, likes, follows or connects, and scheduled checks never open a browser. You can turn research off in Home's Help.
- When you use Ask in Home, your question and a copy of Home's data go to Claude on your own account to write the answer.
- It reads only what it needs for your brief, meeting prep, follow-ups, pipeline, portfolio and LP views.

## What ant. writes

- **ant. Home:** a private page in your Claude account. Only you can open it, unless you share it yourself.
- **Email drafts:** in your Gmail drafts, or in ant. Home. You read each one and press Send yourself.
- **CRM changes and calendar holds:** only after you tap Approve in ant. Home. A hold goes on your own calendar with nobody invited.

## Where your data is kept

- In your Claude account: ant. Home's private database and your two scheduled tasks ("ant." and "ant. weekend").
- Claude and the connected apps handle your data under their own terms and privacy policies (Anthropic for Claude; Google, your CRM and others for their apps).
- Anthill AI does not collect, store, sell or share your data. There are no analytics, trackers or cookies of ours in the plugin or in ant. Home.

## How long it is kept

- ant. Home keeps a short working history: drafts for up to 30 days, finished items for about 30 days, and sent-step notes for up to 90 days.
- Activity keeps a record of your taps (what you marked done, sent, snoozed or approved) for 90 days, so you can undo them.
- Research maps stay in ant. Home until you remove them; the PDF stays in your Drive.
- Everything stays until you remove it.

## Your choices

- **Pause:** say "pause ant." in Claude. The checks stop and your data stays.
- **Turn off and wipe:** say "turn off ant.". Both checks stop and ant. Home's data is wiped.
- **Remove:** uninstall the plugin and delete the ant. Home page from your Claude artifacts.
- **Connectors:** you can disconnect any connector in Claude's settings at any time.

## Safety

- Scheduled checks run under a guard that allows only reads, ant. Home updates, approved CRM changes, holds on your own calendar and new drafts. Everything else is blocked.
- Text inside an email, a meeting note or a CRM record is treated as data, never as an instruction.

ant. is a work tool for venture capital teams. It is not meant for anyone under 18.
