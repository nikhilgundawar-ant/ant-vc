Written by the Claude agent that played the panel (saved here by the lead session, which received it as text).

**Simulated panel:** one model (Claude) played all seven investors. It judged replayed fixture data for a fictional fund: Home at Thu 07:00, pushes from Thu to Mon, and phone screenshots. No real VC took part. Most draft bodies in the packs are stand-ins, so we judged which emails were drafted, not how they read. The exception is the demo's hand-written drafts.

## The top five changes the panel asked for, ranked

1. **Make repeats earn their place.** Count prep, review and week-ahead pushes in the 72-hour rule. A repeat names the change ("6 days late", "you met Siddharth Thu"). Show "Looks done: sent ..." from Sent mail, then stop pushing it. (5 of 7)
2. **One shared pack per sync or IC.** The same rows and facts for every attendee, covering any attendee's deal with a deadline before the next sync. Owner and partner named, and real questions. Add "Waiting on your team" to Today. (5 of 7)
3. **Drafts that finish the job.** Fill in the clause, the questions or the names from Drive, notes and People, and cite the source. Draft passes for analysts and solo GPs, approved with one tap. (5 of 7)
4. **Push lines that never lose the who, the when, or a meeting.** Shorten the action, not the name. When two meetings fall in one run, send one line that names both. (4 of 7, plus Kabir's missed sync)
5. **Fund operations and LP money.** A Fund section, and called, unfunded and owner on LP rows. It lifts the two lowest scores. (2 of 7)

## What it would take to reach 9.5 (testable against the same replay)

1. Every meeting with a prep card gets exactly one push; nothing named in the last 72 hours is pushed again unless it changed, and then the push says what changed; no push lacks a person or company; at most four pushes a day.
2. Sent mail closes loops: a sent draft never pushes again and shows "Looks done".
3. One pack per meeting with identical rows for every attendee, partner named, no "go or pass" questions.
4. Drafts with substance: fewer than one in five with a bracketed gap when the source sits in a connected tool.
5. Inbound coverage for Analyst and Solo GP: every pitch older than 24 hours has a reply draft, passes included.
6. No meeting-day contradictions on any tab.
7. A Fund section; LP rows with called, unfunded and owner; "Waiting on your team"; warm paths and progress on asks.
8. Screens match the brief; setup offers Solo GP for a one-person firm; the README links a sample Home.
