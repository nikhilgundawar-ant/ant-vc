## The top five changes the panel now asks for

1. **Drafts that finish the job (7 of 7):** fill gaps from Drive, notes or People, or say where Instinct looked; never draft for an outcome that has not happened or a meeting already booked; every pitch drafted the first morning, oldest first, to a named founder; associates get replies for their own pitches.
2. **Read the calendar before flagging (5 of 7):** no "Stale", "No next step", "book a call" or "confirm the slot" when a meeting with that company is booked or just happened; close or reword promises whose meeting has passed; label group events by their title.
3. **Pushes that carry news (5 of 7):** the morning line sees every item due that day; a heads-up adds a fact the morning line lacked and keeps "N days late"; no "Review ready: N deals" without a deal.
4. **One shared sync pack and team-wide deal signals (4 of 7):** the same rows and facts for every attendee; write competing-fund signals to the CRM deal on the owner's approval so every attendee's Instinct reads them.
5. **Fund and LP ownership (3 of 7):** a Fund view; called, unfunded and owner on LP rows; one owner and one draft per LP touch.

## What it would take to reach 9.5 (testable against the same replay)

1. One shared sync pack with Sable, Kite Pay, Lumen, Trellis, Rivulet, Gravity and Atlas, owner and partner named; Sable and Trellis Competing on Aditi's Pipeline.
2. Every weekday morning line names an item due that day whenever one exists.
3. No heads-up repeats that morning's line; every push names a person or company; no displaced line in the half hour before a meeting.
4. Calendar truth on every tab and day: no stale, "no next step", "book a call" or "confirm the slot" for a company with a meeting in the past 3 days or the next 7; group events by title; the board deck review closes after the board; Sable "deadline passed" on Monday.
5. No blank in drafts where a connected source holds the fact; otherwise the draft says where Instinct looked; no draft for an outcome that has not happened.
6. Every pitch older than 24 hours drafted by 07:00, oldest first, to a named founder; associates' pitches too; one inbound list on Today.
7. A Fund view; LP rows with called, unfunded and owner; one owner and one draft per LP.
8. Warm paths, an intro draft and progress on each founder ask; the cash warning everywhere.
9. Every heads-up run starts at least 45 minutes before the meeting.
10. Clean words: no broken titles, cut quotes, empty details, unattributed founder words, or hidden instructions in the Copy brief.
11. The demo Home shows every draft the replay makes; the README links a sample Home; the solo replay uses the solo thesis with no partner pack.
