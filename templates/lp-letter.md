# Template: quarterly LP letter

Sections (all required): Fund summary, Portfolio highlights, New investments, Follow-ons and markdowns, Exits, Pipeline and market, Asks.

- Subject: "<quarter> LP letter: draft for your review" ("Q3 2026 LP letter: draft for your review").
- Every number links to its source: the LP list (Carta) for the fund's figures, the founder update for a company's month, the CRM for a deal.
- Fund summary: fund size, committed, called to date and its share, the number of companies, the quarter's key events. Never set paid-in next to called: any gap between them tells every LP that one of them has not paid. A late payment is the LP owner's private note, never the letter's.
- Portfolio highlights: companies at or ahead of their revenue plan with no flag, each with its month in words ("Sep 2026") and the plan. A company with a flag (runway, burn, revenue against plan, churn, a key person leaving) goes on a watch list in the same section, never among the highlights: the reason first, with its figure ("Orbit Treasury: runway below 9 months (8.0 months at September's burn)"), then its month. When a partner said they will write the highlights paragraph, leave it as their bracketed gap.
- New investments: the deals the CRM moved to Portfolio this quarter, with the round and its size; bracket anything to confirm.
- Follow-ons and markdowns, Exits: what the CRM or the fund administrator records; otherwise a bracketed prompt.
- Pipeline and market: open deals by stage from the CRM; the market in the partner's words.
- Asks: what the firm needs from its LPs, in the partner's words; founder asks only when the partner keeps them.
- Partner commentary is left as a prompt: "In your words: what this quarter meant".
