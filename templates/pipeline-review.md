# Template: weekly pipeline review

Under 300 words. Every line links to its deal or thread.

1. Headline: active deals, moves this week, share with no next step.
2. Deals that need a call this week: company, why, the ask.
3. Stale deals: company, reason, one dated next step proposal.
4. Competing funds: company, what the founder said, deadline.
5. Moves this week: company, from and to stage.
