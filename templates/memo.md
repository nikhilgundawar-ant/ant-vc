# Template: investment memo

Headings, in order. Every factual sentence is a linked claim.

1. Summary: the company in two lines, the round, our proposed check.
2. Team: founders, relevant experience, gaps.
3. Problem and product: who buys, why now.
4. Traction: revenue, growth, customers, retention, with dates.
5. Market: size the founders claim and what we can verify, competitors.
6. Business model and unit economics.
7. Round and terms: size, valuation if known, other investors, deadlines.
8. Risks: each with its evidence.
9. Diligence status: what is done, what is open.
10. Open questions.
11. For the investor to write: "Why this could be a fund returner", "What would make us wrong", "Our recommendation".
